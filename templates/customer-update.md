# Customer incident update template

Use the customer's approved channel and named contact list. Keep each update factual, time-stamped (UTC), and consistent with the incident record. Do not include secrets, personal data, speculation, or unapproved technical detail.

## Initial notification

- Customer / incident ID:
- Sent at (UTC) / by:
- Current service and business impact (confirmed scope):
- What is known / what remains under investigation:
- Actions underway and the team coordinating them:
- Customer actions requested (owner and due time), if any:
- Next update time and channel:

## Progress update

- Sent at (UTC) / by:
- Changes since the prior update:
- Current impact and workaround:
- Actions completed / in progress / awaiting customer authorization:
- Risks, dependencies, and decisions needed:
- Next update time:

## Recovery notice

- Recovery time (UTC) and services restored:
- Verification performed and evidence owner:
- Remaining customer impact or monitoring period:
- Actions the customer should take, if any:
- Follow-up / RCA delivery expectation (only if agreed):

## Communication checks

- Confirm recipients and approved channel before sending.
- Separate confirmed facts from hypotheses and state confidence plainly.
- Do not promise a resolution time unless an authorized owner approved it.
- Record the message, timestamp, sender, and customer response in the approved incident system.
- Use the contract and service owner for SLA or service-credit statements.
